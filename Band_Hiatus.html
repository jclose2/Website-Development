The Last Member Standing

Published: August 2026

After months of speculation, Kwimby has officially announced an indefinite hiatus. While fans assumed the break was caused by ordinary "creative differences," the truth turned out to be significantly stranger.

The Great Departure

Sources close to the band confirmed that members gradually disappeared one by one during rehearsals.

  • The drummer left after discovering competitive pickleball and declaring it "far more rhythmically satisfying."
  • The bassist became obsessed with artisanal sourdough bread and has reportedly opened a bakery that only accepts payment in vinyl records. (Well, That explains why our merch store crashed suddenly)
  • The vocalist claimed Mercury was in retrograde and refused to perform until the universe "felt less weird."
  • The keyboard player accidentally joined a progressive bluegrass polka fusion band and never looked back.

With every rehearsal another member vanished, leaving behind only instrument cases, half-finished snack bags, and unanswered group text messages.

Dave Refused to Leave

Lead guitarist Dave Adams remained committed to the band, showing up faithfully to every rehearsal—even after finally realizing nobody else had been there for nearly three months.

Witnesses say Dave completed entire rehearsals by himself, occasionally applauding after guitar solos just to keep morale high.

Neighbors reported hearing conversations like:

"Great idea, everyone." "..." "I agree. Let's play it again."

Dave later admitted that he had forgotten everyone else had already quit and simply assumed they were "running a little late."

The Final Straw

The official breaking point came when Dave accidentally scheduled rehearsal with himself, arrived twenty minutes early, and still managed to be the last person there.

After waiting another hour, he finally accepted reality and voted to place the band on hiatus.

The vote passed unanimously.

Final tally:

  • YES — Dave Adams
  • NO — Nobody
  • ABSTAINED — Everyone else (mostly because they weren't there)

Official Statement

"I looked around the room, counted exactly one band member, and realized I couldn't really call it a band anymore. At that point I was basically just a guy with a guitar and an extension cord."

— Dave Adams

The Future

Although Kwimby is currently on hiatus, Dave has refused to rule out a reunion.

"We've already got the hardest part figured out," he joked. "I know where at least one member lives."

Fans remain hopeful that one day the legendary local rock band will reunite—assuming everyone remembers the rehearsal location.
