Quimby Becomes Kwimby After Discovering... Another Quimby

Published: April 1, 2025

Editor's Note: I swear this is NOT an April Fool's joke. I can't help the timing of events.

What began as an ordinary Tuesday evening rehearsal quickly spiraled into one of the greatest identity crises in local rock history.

The members of Quimby were preparing to launch a website when someone made the bold suggestion:

"Maybe we should Google ourselves."

Nobody was prepared for what happened next.

Within seconds, search results revealed that another band somewhere in the world had already claimed the name Quimby. Witnesses say the rehearsal immediately came to a halt as panic spread throughout the room.

Lead guitarist Dave Adams reportedly stared silently at the computer screen for nearly thirty-six minutes before softly saying,

"Well... that's awkward."

Emergency Band Meeting Declared

The band immediately called what historians now refer to as "The Emergency Q Summit."

Ideas were thrown around at an alarming pace:

  • Quimbee
  • Qwimby
  • Qu1mby
  • Quimby 2: Electric Boogaloo
  • Quimby Reloaded
  • Wiggum
  • That Band Formerly Known as Quimby
  • Quimby 3: Electric Boogalee

After nearly twelve exhausting minutes of discussion, two snack breaks, and one completely unrelated conversation about tacos and dog food, drummer morale began to decline.

The Discovery

Finally, someone quietly asked,

"What if... we just changed the Q to a K?"

The room fell silent.

Birds reportedly stopped chirping. A nearby guitar tuner emitted a single approving beep. One audience member (who wasn't actually there) later claimed they felt "a disturbance in the local music scene."

The vote was unanimous.

Why the Letter K?

According to completely unofficial sources, the reasons included:

  • K is pointier.
  • Pointy letters rock harder.
  • K looked cooler on concert flyers.
  • Nobody wanted to repaint the bass drum more than once.
  • It only required changing one letter on the T-shirts.(Now K-shirts)

Merchandise expenses were reportedly reduced by nearly $11.73, making it one of the most financially responsible decisions in the band's history.

The Aftermath

Fans quickly embraced the new name, although almost everyone continued introducing the band by saying:

"It's Quimby... but with a K."

Ironically, this explanation was required at every performance for the remainder of the band's career.

Despite the confusion, Kwimby continued performing a few more months in 2026, proving that changing one letter was far easier than convincing everyone how to spell it.

Official Band Statement

"We discovered another band named Quimby and decided there was only one logical solution... Change one letter and hope for the best."

— The Members of Kwimby
