From Quimby to Kwimby: A Short but Memorable Journey

Published: July 2026

Like many local bands, Kwimby began as a group of friends who simply enjoyed playing music together. Formed in Richmond, Indiana, the band started by rehearsing in garages and basements before eventually performing at local bars, festivals, and community events throughout eastern Indiana.

Originally performing under the name Quimby(as in Mayor Quimby from the Simpsons), the group quickly gained a following thanks to energetic live performances and a mix of rock covers and original songs. As the band continued to book more shows, they discovered that the name "Quimby" was already associated with other artists and organizations. To establish a more unique identity, the members agreed to change the band's name to Kwimby. Some fans suggested they should instead change the name to Wiggum (as in Police Chief Wiggum) to keep with the spirit of the original Simpsons name. Instead they changed one letter and carried on being just as legendary as before.

Throughout 2025 and into 2026, Kwimby performed at numerous local venues around the Richmond area and neighboring communities. Whether playing neighborhood bars, music halls, or local festivals, the band developed a reputation for entertaining crowds with strong musicianship, humorous stage banter, and a genuine appreciation for their supporters.

Building a Local Following

Rather than chasing national tours, Kwimby focused on building relationships within the local music community. Every performance introduced new listeners while strengthening the band's connection with longtime fans. Many audience members became regulars who attended multiple shows throughout the band's short career.

Lead guitarist Dave Adams became especially known for memorable guitar solos that quickly became a highlight of the band's live performances.

An Unexpected Pause

Just as the band was beginning to discuss recording additional original material, creative differences among the members led to difficult conversations about the band's future. In mid-2026, the members announced that Kwimby would be taking an indefinite hiatus.

Although the announcement disappointed fans, the members emphasized that they remained friends and were grateful for everyone who attended a show, shared a song, or supported the band throughout its journey.

The Legacy of Kwimby

While Kwimby's story may have been brief, the memories created at local venues throughout 2025 and 2026 continue to live on through concert flyers, photographs, videos, and the friendships built around the music. The band's supporters remain hopeful that one day the members may reunite for another performance, maybe even under the name Wiggum as a joke.

Until then, the music—and the memories—continue to remind fans that even local bands can leave a lasting impression.
